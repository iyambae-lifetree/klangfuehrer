How we choose

Up front: researched, not measured by us

The products on this site were not measured in a lab by us and not listened to for weeks in our own living room. Writing “tested” without testing misleads readers. That's why our articles are called comparisons and buying guides, not reviews.

What we do instead is thorough, traceable research, backed by people who build audio software themselves and work with hi-res files, converters and sample rates every day.

How a product makes the list

  1. We read expert sources. Measurements and listening reports from independent sources such as hi-fi magazines and measurement databases. Some of them are German publications; we say so when we cite them. Every technical claim comes from a source, and where sources disagree, we say that too.
  2. We read lots of owner experiences. Recurring complaints (wobbly connectors, noise, unit-to-unit variation) weigh more than a few enthusiastic posts.
  3. We weigh price against performance. We don't recommend anything that is clearly beaten in the same price range.
  4. Who is it for? Every product gets a sentence on who it suits, and who it doesn't.

How often we check

Every page gets reviewed regularly: Is the product still available? Is there a successor? Are the details still right? The “updated” date at the top of each article shows when we last checked.

A note on the English edition

The English edition is adapted from our German guides for readers in the US. Product links go to amazon.com, where models, listings and prices can differ from the German store. Price ranges are given in dollars and are rough guides only.

Found a mistake?

Write to us at the address in the imprint. We take corrections seriously and fix them quickly.
