How this site is funded

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. If you click a link labeled “On Amazon” or “Check price on Amazon” and then buy something, this site receives a small commission from Amazon. Your price stays the same.

Earwise participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to amazon.com.

What this does not mean

  • No manufacturer pays to be on a list or to rank higher.
  • We don't take free gear in exchange for recommendations.
  • The commission rate is the same for every product in a category, so there's no incentive to favor one device over another.

Why there are no prices on the page

Amazon prices change several times a day. A price printed here would almost always be wrong. That's why each product only shows a rough price range; the current price is behind the link.
